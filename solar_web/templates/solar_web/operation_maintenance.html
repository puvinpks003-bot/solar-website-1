O&M Services

Protecting Your Asset Performance for 25 Years

Every SIP INFRA O&M contract guarantees performance through a combination of predictive technology, certified field technicians, and our industry-leading 4-hour fault response SLA.

A //

Thermal Imaging Drone Diagnostics

Biannual automated aerial drone surveys track thermal signatures across the entire array, identifying micro-cracks, cell delamination, hot-spot failures, and bypass diode faults before they cascade into string-level production loss.

B //

Predictive Cloud Performance Tracking

System performance is continuously analyzed against local irradiance and weather data. Machine learning algorithms flag performance deviations down to individual inverter strings, coordinating certified technician response teams within 4 hours.

C //

Panel Cleaning & Vegetation Management

Quarterly scheduled module cleaning using demineralized water systems removes dust, bird fouling, and algae deposits. Studies show cleaning alone recovers 3–7% annual generation — fully managed by our field crews.

D //

Electrical Safety & Insurance Compliance

Annual certified electrical testing including insulation resistance, earth continuity, SPD functionality checks, and RCD trip-time verification — all documented for your insurance provider and CEIG compliance records.
