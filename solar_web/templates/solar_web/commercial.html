Commercial Solar Financing Models

Three proven financial structures for every corporate balance sheet — from full CAPEX ownership to zero-investment OPEX arrangements.

Accelerated 40% Tax Depreciation

Commercial entities write off up to 40% of solar asset investment in year one under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act, significantly lowering capital costs and accelerating break-even horizons to under 5 years.

OPEX / PPA Zero-Investment Solutions

Procure clean energy with zero upfront capital. Pay only for generated solar units via structured Power Purchase Agreements at rates 20–35% below prevailing TANGEDCO utility tariff levels.

Debt-Financed Term Loan Infrastructure

Net-Metering Grid Export

Excess generation exported back to the TANGEDCO grid accumulates unit credits which offset night-time consumption charges. Maximum system utilization, zero generation waste.
