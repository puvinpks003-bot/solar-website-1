Our Process

Standard Compliance Application Workflow

SIP INFRA manages every stage of the regulatory pipeline, so you receive the subsidy without dealing with any paperwork complexity.

Stage 01

Feasibility Registration

Submitting baseline infrastructure data via the national solar portal to receive primary load clearance and sanction validation from your local DISCOM.

Stage 02

Technical Site Clearance

Regional CEIG electrical safety inspectors verify the physical installation — testing interconnect isolation performance, earthing, and protection systems before authorization.

Stage 03

Net Meter Integration

Replacing standard utility meters with advanced bi-directional tracking configurations to accurately measure incoming and outgoing generation unit metrics.

Stage 04

DBT Subsidy Transfer

Submitting final verification metrics to clear straight capital fund transfers directly into your registered bank account — typically within 30 days of commissioning.
